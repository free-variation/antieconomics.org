title: "Ubuntu\\! Ubuntu\\!"
---

It's another story that's been told a thousand times, each with its own variation. In one of the many versions, the initial protagonist was an anthropologist immersed in his fieldwork, seeking to study the customs of an African community. One day, he decided to propose a game to the children of the village where he was living. The anthropologist, having bought a good assortment of fruits and candies in the city, placed them in a basket under a tree. He gathered the children and gave a simple instruction: whoever reached the tree first would win all the fruits and candies.

When the anthropologist signaled the start of the race, a surprising situation unfolded: at first, no one moved. Soon after, the children held hands and walked together towards the tree. After encircling the basket, they sat down and began to distribute the fruits and candies among themselves. When the anthropologist asked why they acted this way, knowing that the fastest runner could have taken everything, they replied in unison, "Ubuntu\! Ubuntu\! How could the one who ran the fastest be happy when all the other children would be sad?"

It's not easy to measure the immense damage that the prevailing individualism in Western societies has caused among us. Aren't those children, who spontaneously concluded that one can hardly be happy when surrounded by the world's many ills, absolutely right?
