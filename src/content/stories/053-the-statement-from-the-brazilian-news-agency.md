title: "The statement from the Brazilian news agency"
---

Some time ago, my friend Suso Jares, who has since passed away, made me aware of a striking and very interesting statement released by a Brazilian news agency on a day indelibly marked in memory. I’m talking about September 11, 2001, the day of the attacks in New York and Washington.

The self-explanatory statement was as follows: '35,615 children died of starvation on September 11, 2001\. Victims: 35,615 children (source: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations, FAO). Location: poor countries. Special television programs: none. Newspaper articles: none. Presidential messages: none. Acts of solidarity: none. Minutes of silence: none. Organized forums: none. Messages from the pope: none. Stock markets: unaffected. Euro: continued as usual. Alert level: zero. Military mobilization: none. Conspiracy theories: none. Main suspects: wealthy countries.'

It’s unnecessary to add that if this happened on September 11, 2011, it happened again on the 12th, on the 13th, reappeared on the 14th... up to today. When we hear from political leaders that the planet’s main problem is what they call *international terrorism*, we have to question the mental balance of so many of them. And about the macabre interests to which they answer.
