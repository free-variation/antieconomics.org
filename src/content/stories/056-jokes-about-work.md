title: "Jokes about work"
---

In a book I published years ago, *La parábola del pescador mexicano* \[The Parable of the Mexican Fisherman\], I included some jokes – perhaps as real as life itself – about the world of work. Here are a few of them.

The first joke succinctly goes: “And how's work?” “Bah, it interrupts my morning...” The second is in the same vein: “And how's work?” “Bah, I don’t have time...” A colleague told me that when she used to call a friend on the phone, she would often ask if she was busy. The usual response was: “No, not at all. I’m working.” Apparently, when that friend wasn’t at work, she never answered the phone, which, I believe, is wise. Finally, another colleague told me about an acquaintance who had only one drunken spree in his life; it lasted from age 14 to 45\. He was diagnosed with such severe cirrhosis that he went into retirement due to disability. When asked if he hadn't drunk too much in life, he replied: “I drank just the right amount. If I drink one more glass, I go to the cemetery. If I drink one less, I’d still be working at the factory.”

Though it has little to do with the spirit of these anecdotes, I can't resist including a quote attributed to George Best, the famous Northern Irish footballer. It goes something like this: "The toughest moment of my life was when I decided to stop drinking. Those were twenty horrible minutes..."
