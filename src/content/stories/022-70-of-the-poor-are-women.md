title: "70% of the poor are women"
---

According to an estimate, 70% of poor people \- and 78% of the illiterate \- on the planet are women. I always insist on this point: these percentages are very telling.  We’re not talking about 52% of women being poor compared to 48% of men. We’re referring to the vast difference between 70 and 30 percent. According to another estimate, which I admit is controversial, women do 67% of the work but receive only 10% of the income.

Large numbers, despite how striking they seem, only go so far.  We need to get down to the nitty-gritty and delve into what they mean. I do so with the help of this text by María González Reyes: "There are women who have never worn a new piece of clothing. There are women who do not have a tap in their homes from which water comes out and yet manage to cook. There are women who have never owned anything. There are women who have never urinated in a bathroom. There are women who have never entered a shopping center. There are women who have never lived in a neighborhood with sewage. There are women who every day wonder if they will get some food. There are women who have never been free to decide what they wanted to do with their bodies. There are women whose daughters and sons die from easily curable diseases. There are women who have never had labor rights. There are women who have never had any rights. There are women who have never thought about the future. There are women for whom the future is not another time, it is another place. There are women who do not live, who survive. There are women who have forgotten to breathe. There are women who live in the other world and who, without gritting their teeth, never forget to take care of the people around them".

I’ll allow myself to draw a quick conclusion: those who think that the age-old problems of symbolic and material marginalization of women are on a fortunate path to resolution are, sadly, turning their backs on reality.
