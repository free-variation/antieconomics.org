title: "One must be depressed"
---

A text by Matt Haig strikes at the heart, in my view, of many of the irrational elements of wealthy societies. He says: “The world is increasingly designed to depress us. Happiness isn’t very good for the economy. If we were happy with what we had, why would we need more? How do you sell an anti-ageing moisturizer? You make someone worry about aging. How do you get people to vote for a political party? You make them worry about immigration. How do you get them to buy insurance? By making them worry about everything. How do you get them to have plastic surgery? By highlighting their physical flaws. How do you get them to watch a TV show? By making them worry about missing out. How do you get them to buy a new smartphone? By making them feel like they are being left behind. “

Given this, staying still, like the Mexican fisherman, becomes a highly respectable act. It is, after all, about being happy with our normal existence, one not marked by the desire to grow and imitate. Those who lead the economy aren’t interested in us living contentedly in our everyday and very personal chaos. They don’t like us going on being ourselves.
