title: "The economy of the libido"
---

I bring up, with an unmistakably humorous tone, a couple of anecdotes related to an economy, that of the libido, which also merits its own anti-capitalist or pre-capitalist version

Whenever I talk about degrowth, I try to emphasize that the logic of family is independent of competition and commodification. It's more commonly associated with donation, gifting, and gratitude. For instance, when parents pay for their children's education, they generally don't do it expecting some future economic return for themselves. But I also like to point out that the perspective of degrowth is neither sad nor gloomy. Most theories of degrowth, paradoxically perhaps, advocate for a general increase in sexual relations, justified by the need to recover the social life we have been squandering in our obsession with production and consumption.

A while back, after finishing a talk on degrowth, a man of around forty approached me and said he particularly liked one of my observations. When I asked which one, he said he really liked the idea that we should *fuck* more, and better – something I hadn't said literally – adding that he had a very serious piece of advice for me. "Say the bit about *fucking* at the beginning of your talk, and then go into all that stuff about self-management and direct democracy…”

Some time later, in a rather solemn setting, I again spoke about degrowth. No one warned me that sitting next to me was a jurist who was also a member of Opus Dei, a former deputy of the Popular Party in Spain, and an opponent of the abortion law. He was the first to speak in the debate. He pointed out \- albeit cordially \- that I had quite a chaotic mess in my head: on one hand, in his view, I defended the institution of the family; and on the other, I was advocating, also in his view, for outright libertinism. He also noted that my position was well-reflected in a sentence attributed to Woody Allen which he couldn't quite remember at the moment. Although I knew it – "Masturbation is fine, but sex is a great way to meet people" – the solemnity of the occasion led me to remain silent. I promise not to do that again.
